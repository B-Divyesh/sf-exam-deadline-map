Privacy, in plain language.

Effective 27 August 2026

Your deck counts, plan settings, generated calendar, and completion history are stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. They are not uploaded to Exam Deadline Map.

Files you choose

CSV imports are read on your device. The app counts rows and does not transmit or retain card content. Exported backup and calendar files are created locally and go only where you save them.

Purchases

If you buy Plan Plus, checkout and license verification are handled by Sociobot’s billing API, with Dodo as merchant of record. The app stores your license token and a cached verification result in localStorage. Payment details are never available to this app.

Network and analytics

The app contains no advertising, behavioral analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or third-party runtime scripts. Your browser may make ordinary requests to this site for application files and, only when licensing is used, to the Sociobot API.

Your control

Use “Your data” in the app to export or delete local plan data. You can remove a saved license separately in Plan Plus. Clearing this site’s browser storage also removes both.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the project repository linked from the app footer.
